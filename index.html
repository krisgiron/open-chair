The Open Chair

A chair, a coffee, and an hour.

If you are between jobs, there is a seat open. Bring whatever you are stuck on and we will work on it together for an hour.

In person around the Bay Area, or over video from anywhere. It is free, and it stays free.

Job searching is lonely work, and most of the advice out there is being sold to you by someone with something to sell. This is just an hour with a person who has read a lot of resumes.

Who this is for

Anyone out of work or about to be. Laid off last week or still looking eight months later. Career changers, first job seekers, people coming back after time away, people who have been quietly applying for months and hearing nothing. You do not need to be in tech and you do not need to know what you want yet.

What we would actually do

  • Go through your resume line by line and fix what is costing you.
  • Work out how to talk about a layoff, a gap, or a short stint without flinching.
  • Figure out why your search is stalling, which is usually somewhere specific rather than everywhere.
  • Practice the interview questions you are dreading, out loud, until they stop being scary.
  • Look at where you are applying and whether it is the right list.
  • Or just talk. Some hours are for that, and those hours count.

What this is not

  • Not a paid service, a funnel, or a free session with an upsell at the end.
  • Not recruiting. I have no jobs to place you into.
  • Not a guarantee. I cannot get you hired, and anyone who says they can is selling something.
